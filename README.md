# [Akratomate](https://arrangatan.itch.io/akratomate) - a game by hamdivazim and Arrangatan

A malfunctioning robot gets dumped into a well and falls into an underground pit. Fight waves of slimes and upgrade in order to face more powerful waves. Its a top-down shooter with a gimmick to do with aiming - Your barrel spins around constantly and tapping spacebar shoots and also switches the rotation of the barrel (Hence you are pretty out of control of your aim). You are the enemy as you fight yourself (your controls). Movement is just WASD or arrow keys. Esc to access menu

Made over 2 days by Arrangatan (programming, art, SFX) and hamdivazim (programming, art, title-art). Have fun!
